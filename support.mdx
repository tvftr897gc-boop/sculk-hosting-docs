---
title: "Get help"
description: "Find support and prepare the details needed to troubleshoot."
---

For help with a server, use **Staff Support** in the panel or [join the Sculk Hosting Discord](https://discord.gg/c94S7eSuem). The dashboard also links to **Contact support** and **Uptime Status**.

When you ask for help, include the server ID from **Overview**, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and roughly when it started. Relevant **Console** errors and the steps you already tried can speed up troubleshooting. Check logs for passwords, tokens, database credentials, and personal information before sharing them.

For invoices, plans, and payment, use the [billing portal](https://my.sculkhosting.com) linked from the panel dashboard.
